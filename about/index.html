About Survival on the Record

Survival on the Record: true survival stories minute by minute from logs, rescue reports and survivors' own words. Adrift, trapped, lost, found.

Who makes this

Survival on the Record is a YouTube channel published by Vision Media. We research each story and write each script. The narrator is a text-to-speech voice. Every video description says so.

How we work

Every hour on the clock comes from a published account we name: the survivor's own book, the rescue report, the court record. No dialogue is invented. Sources are listed in each video's description and at the end of every article. Images are public domain, openly licensed or our own, and the credits page lists them for each video.
